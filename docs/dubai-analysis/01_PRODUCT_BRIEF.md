# 01 — Product Brief

## Product name

**Dubai Market Planning Studio**  
Client-facing title in the app: **Market Planning for Prophunters**

## One-line proposition

A self-serve planning studio that recommends an initial international buyer-market portfolio for Dubai off-plan real estate, then lets a client adjust lead targets, media investment, countries, languages and channel allocation to understand the resulting acquisition plan and its likely timeline.

## The problem to solve

A client needs to understand:

- What monthly media investment is sensible for the plan they want to run.
- What lead target that investment could support using available CPL benchmarks.
- Which international markets are worth prioritizing, testing or monitoring.
- How a language-capable sales team changes the reachable market opportunity.
- How investment is divided between Meta and Google.
- Why sales do not necessarily happen in the same month as lead generation, and what the historical sales-cycle evidence can responsibly tell them.

The product is a decision-support tool, not a guarantee of leads, buyers, transactions or returns.

## Product experience

The experience should have three connected layers:

### 1. Recommended plan

The app opens with a pre-built recommendation based on the current research data and the client's serviceable markets. It should not ask the client to build a strategy from zero.

The recommendation includes:

- A starter group of recommended countries.
- A clear explanation of why each country is included.
- The client-provided regional CPL reference where one exists.
- The evidence quality and date for each market signal.
- A suggested monthly lead target and the investment implied by the available benchmark.
- A neutral starting Meta/Google allocation, clearly distinguished from an evidence-backed optimum.
- A language coverage view based on the languages agents can actually support.

### 2. Customize the plan

The client can:

- Choose either a monthly media-budget commitment or a monthly raw-lead target.
- Choose from suggested planning presets or enter a custom value.
- Keep the recommended market portfolio or customize it.
- Multi-select individual countries from a searchable worldwide list.
- Add or remove regions and countries without losing the recommended starting point.
- Select the languages their agents can support, with proficiency levels.
- Adjust the Meta/Google media allocation.
- Compare the new plan with the recommended plan.

### 3. Understand the forecast

The result should show:

- Monthly media investment.
- Target or estimated raw leads, depending on the selected planning mode.
- Estimated blended CPL benchmark or range.
- New/learning versus mature campaign comparison.
- Country/region allocation and its contribution to total budget and leads.
- Meta/Google spend allocation, with channel lead estimates only when channel CPL data exists.
- Sales-cycle timing insights, with numeric sales forecasts disabled until the conversion-rate data is validated.
- A transparent explanation of assumptions and limitations.

## Intended audience

A Dubai off-plan real-estate client or decision-maker who may understand marketing budgets but should not need to understand ad-platform mechanics, attribution jargon or statistical modelling to use the product.

The client should feel guided by a knowledgeable market strategist. Avoid making them feel they are being tested on marketing knowledge.

## What this product is not

- Not a generic analytics dashboard.
- Not a static PDF report presented as a web page.
- Not a CRM, campaign manager or ad-account control panel.
- Not an investment-return or property-price prediction engine.
- Not a lead-to-sale guarantee.
- Not a country ranking based only on GDP, population or internet usage.
- Not an interface that requires the client to choose every market and every assumption unaided.
- Not a clone of another VoiceEstate workspace screen or a forced Prophunters brand treatment.

## Success criteria

A first-time client should be able to answer these questions without assistance:

1. What plan is being recommended and why?
2. What does the plan cost per month?
3. How many raw leads is it designed to target or expected to generate?
4. Which countries and languages are included?
5. What is known about the CPL in those markets, and how reliable is that information?
6. How is the budget divided across Meta and Google?
7. What is known—and not yet known—about the sales timeline?
8. What changes when they adjust one input?

## Key product rules

- The recommended portfolio is pre-selected on first load.
- Every input has suggested options plus a custom override.
- The client does not select campaign maturity. The forecast compares learning and mature outcomes automatically wherever both are benchmarked.
- Markets can be multi-selected and grouped by region.
- Language capacity is a serviceability filter and market-expansion signal, not an assumption that every speaker is a buyer.
- Budget/CPL/lead calculations must be mathematically consistent and explainable.
- Unverified values must never be silently filled with invented values.
- Forecast output must update from the current input state; controls must not be decorative.
- The first implementation approval is the reusable KPI card. Do not implement the entire dashboard at once.
